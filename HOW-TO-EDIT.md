# How to edit your portfolio (no code)

## Log in
1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org** (bookmark it).
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** and pick this repository.
3. Only GitHub accounts with access to this repository can edit, so that's just you.

## What you can edit
| In the editor | What it changes on the site |
|---|---|
| **About me, skills, education & contact** | Name, title under your name, tagline, About text, availability badge, city, portrait, education, skills, resume PDF, email, LinkedIn |
| **Projects** | Every project: name, photos, description, fabrics, time taken, cost, date |

Click **Save** after editing. The live site updates in about 1–2 minutes.

## Adding a project
1. Open **Projects** → **Add an entry**.
2. **Final look photo**: the finished garment. This is the first image people see.
3. **Concept development images**: sketches, mood boards, fabric tests, drapes, in the order you want them shown.
4. Fill in description, fabrics, time taken and date.
5. **Total cost** is optional. Leave it empty and it won't appear.
6. Tick **Show on home page** for your best 3. If none are ticked, the first 3 in the list are shown.
7. Drag entries up or down to change their order.

## Removing the sample projects
Open **Projects** and delete the four "Sample Project" entries once your real projects are in.

## Photo tips (keeps the site fast)
- Use JPG photos around **2000 px on the longest side** and under **1 MB** each. Resize on your phone, or use squoosh.app.
- Portrait-shaped photos (4:5) look best on the project cards.

## Resume
In **About me…** upload a PDF in the **Resume** field. Until you do, the button says "Request resume" and opens an email to you.
